ImageRelay — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-20

ImageRelay ("the extension") is an independent browser extension that automates batch image generation on the ChatGPT website. This policy explains what the extension does and does not do with your data.

Summary

ImageRelay does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. Everything stays on your own computer.

What the extension stores

  • Your prompts, queue, and settings — saved locally in your browser's own storage (chrome.storage.local and IndexedDB). They never leave your device.
  • Generated images — downloaded directly to your computer's Downloads folder. The extension does not keep a copy anywhere else.

What the extension does NOT do

  • It does not send your prompts, images, settings, browsing history, or any other data to the developer or any third-party server.
  • It does not run analytics or tracking.
  • It does not create an account or require a login with the developer.

Network activity

The extension's only interaction with the network is acting on your behalf inside the chatgpt.com page you are already logged into — typing prompts and collecting the images ChatGPT generates, exactly as you would do by hand. It makes no requests to any server operated by the developer.

Permissions

  • downloads — to save generated images to your Downloads folder.
  • storage — to keep your prompt queue and settings on your device.
  • alarms — to time the delay between image generations.
  • sidePanel, scripting — to show the extension's interface and operate the ChatGPT page.
  • Access to chatgpt.com — the only website the extension operates on.

Affiliation

ImageRelay is an independent tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by OpenAI. "ChatGPT" is a trademark of OpenAI.
